# Beacon dark workspace and program guides

The default appearance is Midnight. The workspace header also offers Graphite and Deep Blue. All three retain a black/blue identity, readable pale text and semantic status colors. The selection is a device-local preference; evidence stays in the existing persistent workspace. The hosted application, portable client, sign-in page, marketing page, dialogs and tables share the dark direction.

## Three design directions

| Direction | Visual treatment | Useful next design work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight | Near-black canvas, layered slate surfaces, blue controls | A central change feed linking source events, findings and affected reports |
| Graphite | Neutral charcoal panels with restrained blue emphasis | A dense reviewer workspace with evidence comparison and approval queues |
| Deep Blue | Navy surfaces and stronger blue boundaries | A topology view linking assets, controls and evidence, with a shared matrix view |

These are selectable color treatments, not separate products. Browser visual QA has not been performed. Build checks do not establish visual or accessibility conformance.

It links to the working document mapper, infrastructure workspace and verification/report studio. ## Recommended implementation order

1. **Approval workflow:** policy interpretations, scope and evidence contracts need named reviewers, separation of duties, exception expiry and version-bound approval. Keep model proposals unapproved until that workflow completes.
2. **One trusted connector path:** establish workload identity, signed collection jobs, source-byte integrity, declared populations and freshness; demonstrate one real drift finding from collection through validated recovery.
3. **Persistent operations:** supervised scheduling, independent missed-run alerts, reliable delivery with redacted payloads, retries and receipts, and production retention/restore.
4. **One complete reporting path:** select the first applicable program and deliver its full schema, evidence citations, reviewer sign-off and reproducible export before claiming broad package readiness.
5. **Controlled trust-center projection:** publish approved fields with freshness and withdrawal behavior; retain sensitive evidence behind audience-specific access.

The implementation does not connect live organization services, send notifications, approve findings or generate certification opinions. These remain explicit production work.

Primary references are linked in each guide: FedRAMP CR26 certification and SDR rules, AICPA SOC resources, DoD CMMC resources, and ISO's 27001/42001 standard pages. Applicability, exact editions and current contractual obligations require program-specific review.
